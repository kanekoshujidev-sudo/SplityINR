# Privacy Policy for Splity INR

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Splity INR ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

## No Account Required

Splity INR does not require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identification information to use the core features of the app.

## No Data Retention

We do not store, save, or retain any of the bill-splitting or usage data you enter into the app on our servers. Any information you enter is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or stored by us.

## Advertising

This app displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These advertising partners may automatically collect certain information to serve and measure ads, including:

- Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
- IP address
- Device information (device model, operating system version)
- General app usage and interaction data related to ads

This data is collected and processed by the advertising network in accordance with their own privacy policies, not directly by us. We do not have access to, and do not store, this advertising data ourselves.

For more information on how Google AdMob handles data, please see:
[https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android).

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties, other than the advertising network described above, which operates independently under its own privacy policy.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
